? How we spot trends ▶

Signal sources

  • Price leadership within a cluster of related names
  • Capital flows — ETF launches & 13F initiations
  • Sell-side initiation clusters on a theme
  • Structural catalyst — technology, regulation or supply
  • Rising language frequency in filings & earnings calls

Promotion criteria

  • An investible basket actually exists
  • Three-bucket confirmation: price + flows + tailwind
  • Persistence over several weeks, not a spike
  • Distinct from themes already in the book
  • A credible multi-year runway

Graduate & retire

  • Need ~3 of 5 criteria met to graduate to investible
  • Radar items are re-scored each week
  • Track each call's outcome: climbing, graduated or fad
Retire rule: when a theme shows no new evidence for ~2 months and its trend is rolling over, it moves Fading → Retired. Cyclical themes that cool but could re-fire are parked as Dormant for revival-watch, not deleted.
